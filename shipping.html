Shipping & FAQ

How your order gets to you.

We pack and dispatch from the studio ourselves. Nothing is charged until you have approved a written quotation with freight included.

Step by step

The whole process, in three steps.

01

Choose your pieces

Add what you like to the inquiry and send it on WhatsApp or email. No account, no card, no commitment.

02

We quote, you approve

Within one working day you get availability, freight to your city and the landed total — duties shown separately, never hidden.

03

Pay and we ship

Approve the quote and we send a secure payment link. In-stock pieces leave the studio in 5–7 days with tracking.

Returns

If something is not right.

Arrived damaged
Send a photograph within 7 days of delivery and we will replace the piece or refund it, including freight — whichever you prefer. We do not ask you to ship ceramics back.
Changed your mind
Unused pieces in their original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Return freight is on you unless the piece was faulty, and the refund is issued once it arrives back intact.
Custom or made-to-order pieces
Made-to-order and personalised pieces are not returnable, because they were produced specifically for you. We confirm dimensions and finish by message before starting production.
Wrong item or short delivery
Message us the same day with the packing slip and we ship the missing piece at our cost, usually within 48 hours.

Questions

The ones people actually ask.

Why can I not pay on the website?
Because freight and duties vary too much across our markets to charge blindly at checkout. We would rather quote you a real landed price than take a payment and then ask for more. Once you approve, payment is a one-click secure link.
Which currencies do you accept?
We invoice in USD by default, and can invoice in AED, SAR or MYR. Prices on the site convert for reference using indicative rates; the exact amount is fixed in your quotation.
Do you do wholesale or interior projects?
Yes — for hotels, restaurants and design studios we produce larger runs and can adjust glaze and dimensions. Send a brief through the contact page and mention quantities.
Can I see the piece before ordering?
We send additional photographs or a short video of the actual unit on request, particularly for pieces where glaze or grain varies. Just ask on WhatsApp.

Still unsure about freight?

Send us your city and a list of pieces — we will come back with a landed price the same day.
