Our story

We make slowly, on purpose.

MARAYA began in a two-room studio with a single kiln and a stubborn belief: that the objects you touch every day should be made by people, not machines. Every piece is thrown, woven or finished by hand, in runs small enough that the maker still knows each one.

We work directly with six family-run workshops across the region — no middle layer, no wholesale markup, no seasonal churn. When a batch sells out, it is genuinely gone until the next firing.

Good to know

Ordering, simply.

Three steps: choose, send, confirm. We handle the rest by WhatsApp.

How does ordering work if I cannot pay on the site?
You add pieces to your inquiry, send it on WhatsApp or by email, and we reply with a written quotation — availability, freight to your city, and the landed total. Once you approve, we send a secure payment link or bank details. Nothing is charged before that.
How long until my order arrives?
In-stock pieces leave the studio in 5–7 days. Transit is typically 3–5 days to Malaysia, Singapore and Thailand, 5–9 days to Indonesia and the Philippines, and 4–8 days to the Gulf. Made-to-order pieces add two weeks.
Are duties and taxes included?
They are shown separately in your quotation so there are no surprises at the door. For the Gulf we can ship duty-paid on request; for Southeast Asia, import thresholds vary by country and we will flag anything that applies.
What if something arrives damaged?
Send us a photo within 7 days of delivery and we replace it or refund it — whichever you prefer. Ceramics are double-boxed with moulded pulp inserts precisely because we would rather not have this conversation.

Tell us what you are furnishing.

Send a room, a mood board or a list. We will come back with a selection and a single landed price.
