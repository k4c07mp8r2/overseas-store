Shipping & lead time
In-stock pieces are dispatched in 5–7 days. Transit to Southeast Asia is typically 3–9 days and 4–8 days to the Gulf, tracked door to door. Made-to-order pieces add roughly two weeks.
Duties & taxes
Shown separately in your quotation so nothing appears at the door. For Gulf destinations we can ship duty-paid on request.
Returns
Send a photo within 7 days of delivery if anything arrives damaged, and we replace or refund it. Unused pieces can be returned within 14 days; return freight is on you unless the piece was faulty.
